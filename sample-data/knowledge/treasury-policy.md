# Treasury Policy

Owner: Group Treasurer · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Document ID: FIN-TR-001

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Contoso manages liquidity, payments, bank relationships, foreign exchange
and counterparty risk.

## Liquidity and Cash Forecasting

A rolling 13-week cash forecast is produced weekly from AP open payables, AR open receivables,
payroll and debt service. Forecast accuracy is measured against actuals with a target variance of
±5% at week four. Minimum operating liquidity is USD 75 million across operating accounts and the
committed revolving facility.

## Payment Execution and Controls

- Payment runs execute on Tuesday and Thursday; off-cycle payments require Treasury approval and are
  limited to supplier stoppage, statutory and emergency situations.
- All outbound payments are released under dual control. The payment file is prepared by Accounts
  Payable and released by two authorised Treasury signatories.
- Payments above USD 250,000 require Group Treasurer approval; payments above USD 1,000,000 require
  CFO approval.
- Positive pay and account validation services are enabled on all disbursement accounts.
- Bank detail changes for suppliers follow control FIN-SOX-AP-04 and require independent callback
  verification before the first payment.

## Discounts and Cost of Capital

The corporate weighted average cost of capital used for early payment discount decisions is 9.5%
annualised. An early payment discount is taken when the annualised yield of the discount exceeds
9.5%; otherwise the invoice is paid at term.

## Foreign Exchange

Transactional exposures above USD 500,000 equivalent are hedged with forward contracts of up to
twelve months. Hedge accounting designation is documented at inception. Speculative positions are
prohibited.

## Counterparty and Bank Relationship Management

Cash may only be held with counterparties rated A- or better. Concentration with any single
counterparty is limited to 30% of group cash. Bank account opening and closing requires Group
Treasurer approval and is recorded in the bank account register reviewed quarterly.

## Intercompany Funding

Intercompany loans are executed under a master intercompany agreement at arm's length rates set
quarterly by Treasury and Tax. Cash pooling participation is reviewed annually.
